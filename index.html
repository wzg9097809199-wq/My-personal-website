About

I'm a designer who writes production code, which mostly means I don't hand off work I haven't tested myself. Ten years in, split between in-house product teams and independent projects for small studios.

Recent focus has been on booking and scheduling tools — the unglamorous flows that decide whether someone finishes a task or gives up halfway through.

Outside of client work, I teach a weekend typography class and keep a small notebook of interface details worth stealing.
